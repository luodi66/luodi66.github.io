Code Practice Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Code Practice (编程练习, “the App”) is a coding practice tool provided by the developer Hongqun Luo (“we”). We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. We do not collect any personal information

The App requires no registration or sign-in. It does not collect, upload or share any of your personal information, including your name, phone number, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos or usage history. The App contains no ads and no third-party analytics, advertising, push notification or sign-in SDKs.

2. Your code and settings stay on your device

The code, homework and preferences you create are stored only on your device, and we cannot see them. Code is compiled and run entirely offline on your device and is never sent to any server.

Your homework can also be found in the Files app under “On My iPhone” (or “On My iPad”) → “Code Practice”. Deleting the App also deletes this data. To keep it, first use “Export All Homework (Backup)” in the App.

3. Network

Editing, compiling and running code do not require an internet connection, and the App does not send data to any server. Only when you tap a link on the About page, such as Help or an open-source component, will your browser open that website. The website’s operator is responsible for how it handles information.

4. System permissions

The App does not request access to the camera, microphone, location or contacts, and it never reads your photos.

Only when you export code as an image and choose “Save Image” in the share sheet will the system ask whether the App may add photos. The App saves only the image you exported.

When you import a file, the App reads only the file you selected. When you export or share, the content is sent to the app you choose (such as WeChat, Mail or Files), and that app is responsible for how it is handled afterward.

5. Children

The App is suitable for students. Because the App collects no personal information, no personal information is collected from children either.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version in the App and on our website and update the date at the top.

7. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy, please email 849563983@qq.com.
